What is RL4AA'27?

We are pleased to announce RL4AA'27, the fifth workshop of the series organised by the Reinforcement Learning for Autonomous Accelerators (RL4AA) Collaboration. After four very successful workshops (KIT in 2023, Salzburg in 2024, DESY in 2025, and Liverpool in 2026), RL4AA'27 will be hosted by SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory in Menlo Park, California, from 20 to 23 April 2027.

RL4AA brings together the reinforcement learning and accelerator physics communities to share results, practical insights from the field, and the big questions ahead for real-world RL. Expect engaging keynotes, invited and contributed talks, a lively poster session, and a hands-on coding challenge.

Scope

Submissions are not limited to particle accelerators. We welcome any work on sequential decision-making in real-world settings (e.g., safety, sample efficiency, sim-to-real, partial observability). A set of student talk slots will be reserved.

Coding challenge

Join a team to tackle an accelerator-themed RL task. We will form balanced teams that mix newcomers and experienced contributors. You’ll submit results to a live leaderboard as you go, improve your model, and we will wrap up with awards for the top two teams. More details will follow.

Who should attend

Whether you are deep into RL research or just starting and curious, there’s something for you, from introductory lectures on the first day to advanced talks.

Registration

The event is free, but places are limited. Registration and the call for abstracts will open soon – see the registration page. Please register early to secure your spot.

We look forward to welcoming you to SLAC in 2027!
