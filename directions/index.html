Venue

RL4AA'27 will take place at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, 2575 Sand Hill Road, Menlo Park, California, close to Stanford University. Details on the meeting room and site access will follow.

Getting There

The nearest airports are San Francisco International (SFO), San Jose Mineta International (SJC), and Oakland International (OAK). We recommend arranging your travel early. More information about airports, local transport and lodging is available on SLAC’s Traveling to SLAC – Visitor Information page.

Where to Stay

We recommend the Stanford Guest House, where a block of rooms has been reserved for workshop participants. Please book while rooms remain available:

  • Online: book with the RL4AA group rate
  • By phone: call the front desk at (650) 926-2800 and mention the group code RL4AA
